The people behind our legacy

Guided by a founding vision since 1983 and led with dedication every day.

Late Smt. Kanta Adlakha

Founder · In loving memory

In 1983, Smt. Kanta Adlakha planted a small seed with a big dream – a school where every child would be cared for like family and taught to walk the path of knowledge and values. Her warmth, discipline and devotion to children remain the foundation of everything we do. Every lamp lit in our classrooms carries her light forward.

Dear parents and students, for more than four decades our family has carried forward a promise made by our founder – to give every child an education rooted in knowledge, discipline and kindness.

As we grow, we continue to invest in better classrooms, modern facilities and new opportunities, while holding on to the values that make Vivek Model a second home for our children. I warmly invite you to become a part of our school family.

At Vivek Model, we believe every child is unique and full of potential. Our teachers work closely with each student – encouraging curiosity, building confidence and nurturing good habits from the very first day of school.

Along with strong academics, we celebrate festivals, sports, arts and values like kindness and respect, so that our children grow into well-rounded individuals. Together with parents, we look forward to shaping a bright future for every child.
