About us

A school family built on values

The Vivek Model journey began in 1983 with a simple belief: every child deserves an education that builds both the mind and the heart. That belief led to our Maruti Kunj campus in 2001 – Vivek Model Sr. Sec. School – and to our Sohna branch, Vivek Model School, in 2019.

Over 40+ years, more than 10,000 students have studied with the Vivek Model family. Today, both campuses share the same spirit: caring teachers, an NCERT-based curriculum following the CBSE pattern, and a calendar full of activities, festivals and celebrations where every child gets a chance to shine.

Our Vision

To light the path of every child from darkness to knowledge, raising responsible citizens with strong character and a love for learning.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable education in a safe, joyful environment – balancing academics, sports, arts and values.

FAQs

Questions parents ask

Everything you need to know about Vivek Model Sr. Sec. School (VMSSS) and Vivek Model School (VMS), Sohna.

Where is Vivek Model Sr. Sec. School (VMSSS) located?

Vivek Model Sr. Sec. School is at Vatika Kunj, Maruti Kunj, Gurugram, Haryana 122102. Our branch, Vivek Model School (VMS), is near Chungi No. 1, Nangli Mod, Sohna, Gurugram, Haryana 122103.

Which board is the school affiliated to?

The Maruti Kunj campus is affiliated to the Board of School Education Haryana (Haryana Board), Affiliation No. 5035. Teaching follows an NCERT-based curriculum on the CBSE pattern.

Which classes and streams are available?

Maruti Kunj offers Nursery to Class 12 with Science, Commerce and Humanities streams in Classes 11–12. The Sohna branch offers Nursery to Class 10.

Since when has Vivek Model School been running?

The Vivek Model family has been educating children since 1983 – over 40 years – and more than 10,000 students have studied with us.

How can I take admission for session 2027–28?

Admissions are open. Fill the enquiry form on this website, WhatsApp us at +91 98111 97509 (Maruti Kunj) or +91 98111 97029 (Sohna), or visit the school office Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 12 PM.

What are the school timings?

School runs Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The office and admission desk are open 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
